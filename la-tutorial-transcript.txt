[0:07] WELCOME TO L.A. LASTS A LIFETIME

L.A. Lasts A Lifetime is an A to Z encyclopedia of Los Angeles County, from Acton to Zuma. It describes 4,900 places: landmarks, buildings, restaurants, shops, murals, homes and more. It's organized like a reference book, in two parts. Part One, the A to Z of Placenames, lists the names of areas: neighborhoods, cities, unincorporated communities, historic districts, and Historic Preservation Overlay Zones.

Part Two, the A to Z of Places, is the main directory of individual places. In both parts, you can browse alphabetically or search for something specific.

[5:25] NAVIGATION AND ACCESSIBILITY

As you scroll, a bar stays at the top of the screen. The underline shows which part you're in. Beside it are the number of places showing, and a Reset filters button. In the letter bar, a dark square marks the letter you're reading.

Everything also works from the keyboard. Press Tab to move from one control to the next. A dark outline shows where you are. In the area box, the up and down arrow keys move through the list.

Escape closes it. Type a few letters, and Enter chooses an area. On a phone, the page rearranges itself into a single column with larger text. The search box, the area box, and the letter bar sit together at the top, so you can search and browse with your thumb.

If your device is set to reduce motion, the site respects that. The opening number doesn't count up, the featured name stays still, and jumps happen instantly, without gliding. And if your device uses dark mode, the site follows it.
